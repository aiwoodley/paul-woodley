01 / About

Curiosity that turned into a career.

I'm a West Palm Beach native, now in Broward County. I'm a first-generation Florida State University graduate, and I'm a twin — my brother Peter and I build everything together.

I've always wanted to know how things actually work: the machine, the network, the system underneath the system. That curiosity became my job, and then it became our businesses. I take apart what other people throw away, learn it deeply, and turn it into something someone else can use.

I'm a big-picture guy. Every piece of what I do — engineering, DigiLabs, the home network builds, the miles — is proof of one idea: it always seems impossible until it's done.
